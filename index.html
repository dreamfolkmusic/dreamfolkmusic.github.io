SIDE B / THE DUO

A FICTIONAL WORLD. HUMAN SONGWRITING.

Two machines.
One shared
daydream.

Somewhere among the tape reels and tangled cables, two machines discover something they were never programmed to feel: music.

That’s the fictional world of Dreamfolk: a male and female retro humanoid robot duo, finding a little warmth between strings and circuitry.

Behind that world is an independent music project built on original, human-written songs and produced using AI. The characters are imagined. The songwriting starts with a person.
