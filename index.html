Between strings & circuitry

Two machines. One shared daydream.

Somewhere among the tape reels and tangled cables, two machines discover something they were never programmed to feel: music.

That’s the fictional world of Dreamfolk: a male and female retro humanoid robot duo, finding a little warmth between strings and circuitry.

Behind that world is an independent music project built on original, human-written songs and produced using AI. The characters are imagined. The songwriting starts with a person.

Acoustic intimacy meets electronic imagination. Indie folk, art pop and dream pop, with a current of electronica running underneath.

The guitarist / Male robot

A quiet kind of electricity.

Acoustic guitar, instruments and backing vocals. Weathered ivory, muted teal and a thoughtful, quietly playful spirit.

The vocalist / Female robot

A spark in the circuitry.

Lead vocals and multiple instruments. Warm ivory, copper and brass, with a spirited imagination and a vintage soul.

Human-written. AI-produced. Robot characters and imagery are fictional.
