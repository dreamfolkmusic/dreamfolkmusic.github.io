# Dreamfolk redesign

The site is a static GitHub Pages site served from the repository root. The custom domain, canonical URL, sitemap, robots rules, existing music/social/contact URLs and Cloudflare analytics are preserved.

## Creative decisions

- Editorial record-sleeve layout with vintage ivory, warm charcoal, burnt orange, brass and muted teal.
- Male/female retro humanoid robot duo; no character names assigned.
- Debut single remains coming soon, with no invented track title, release date or audio player.
- Fictional character story is distinguished from the human-written, AI-produced music project.
- The two previous conversation image paths were unavailable. Replacement studio artwork was generated with the built-in image generation tool, then optimized to 1536 × 1024 JPEG (desktop/social) and 800 × 533 JPEG (mobile). Original source retained outside the repository.
- The previous mountain artwork and temporary vector duo are no longer used. Historical assets remain available for archival use.

## Artwork prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a cinematic high-quality 3D promotional photograph for Dreamfolk, a male and female retro humanoid robot indie music duo, in a warm mid-century analog recording studio. Wide landscape 3:2 composition. Both characters visible waist-up, left male weathered ivory and muted teal metal, rounded expressive illuminated amber eyes, relaxed vintage brown jacket, holding an acoustic guitar. Right female ivory robot with copper brass details and sculpted retro bob hairstyle, expressive mechanical feminine face and amber eyes, burnt orange 1960s clothing beside a vintage silver microphone. Approachable adult musicians, beautifully detailed real weathered materials, not toys, no human skin. Reel to reel tape machines and wood acoustic panels behind them, tungsten lamps, soft film grain, atmospheric warm amber shadows and muted teal. Strong editorial indie record sleeve photography, intimate quiet moment, no text, no lettering, no logo, no watermark. Keep both faces fully inside central 70 percent for responsive cropping.
